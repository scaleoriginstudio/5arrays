Product Description

Built for every school day. The 5Arrays School Jumper is made from 100% responsibly sourced cotton, soft against the skin, warm through winter, and tough enough to keep up with the chaos of a school day. Knitted with a fine finish so it looks as good on the last day of term as it did on the first.

Size and Fit

Unisex regular fit.
Available from Junior Age 3-4 through to Adult 2XL, sized to suit the whole school from first year to final year.

Features

  • Stay Smart Technology: elastane-reinforced neck, hem, and cuffs hold their shape wash after wash, so it always looks put-together.

100% responsibly sourced cotton.
Machine washable, low tumble dry. No fuss.
Worn on its own or under a school blazer, it works either way.
